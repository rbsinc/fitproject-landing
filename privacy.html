Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-11

Short version

Fit Project does not collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Fit Project is an independent app built and operated by a single developer. Contact: support@fitproject.app

2. Data we collect

None. All workout data — exercises, sets, reps, weight, rest periods, RIR, equipment configuration, and your profile (experience level, goal, frequency, session duration) — is stored only on your device. We do not have a server, we do not have a copy, and we cannot access it.

Fit Project does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, or any identifier. We do not collect device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry. There is no third-party SDK in the app for tracking, advertising, or analytics.

3. How your data is used

Your workout data is used by the app, on your device, to show your workout history, generate routines via Smart Rotation, and track your progress. None of it leaves your device.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party. There are no third-party processors with access to your workout data — because there is no server holding any.

5. Data retention

Your workout data lives on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no remote copy to delete.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf — you retain full control of your data on your device. If you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under GDPR / UK GDPR; in California, under the CCPA. Email support@fitproject.app with any privacy questions.

7. Children

Fit Project is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children — and given the policy above, we do not collect data from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy (for example, if we ever add cloud sync), we will update the "Last updated" date and notify users in-app where required.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: support@fitproject.app
