How it works

Set up once. Train forever.

One minute of setup gives you personalized workouts for as long as you train.

01

Configure your profile

Set your experience level, primary goal (strength, hypertrophy, or endurance), weekly frequency, session duration, and available equipment. Takes about a minute.

02

Generate or build

Use Smart Rotation to auto-generate a weekly routine, or create your own templates. Either way, just hit Start Workout when you're ready.

03

Log and progress

Track every set during your session. Log your reps in reserve. Watch your strength curves climb. The more data you give it, the smarter your workouts become.

FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers.

Is FitProject really free?+
Yes. FitProject is free to use — no subscription, no paywall on core features. It was built as a passion project by a developer who was tired of paying monthly fees just to log workouts. The workout logging and smart generation features you see today are free, and the goal is to keep it that way.
How does Smart Rotation work?+
Smart Rotation generates your weekly workout plan based on your profile settings — experience level, primary goal, available equipment, weekly frequency (2-7 days), and session duration (15-120 minutes). It creates a balanced split (push, pull, lower body, etc.) and rotates through it, factoring in what muscle groups you've recently trained and your progression over time. It's a rule-based engine that runs entirely on your phone — same inputs, same workout. No AI, no black box.
What can I track during a workout?+
For each exercise you can track sets (including warm-up sets), reps, weight, and rest periods. After completing an exercise, you rate your effort using Reps in Reserve (RIR). The app also tracks your total session time, total volume, personal records, and shows your strength curve progression over time.
What data do you collect?+
FitProject is privacy-first. Your workout data is never sold to advertisers or shared with third parties. Your fitness data is yours alone.
Can I use my own workouts instead of generated ones?+
Absolutely. You can create custom workout templates, save completed workouts as templates, and build your own weekly routine from scratch. Smart Rotation is there when you want it, but you're never locked into it.
What equipment is supported?+
FitProject supports bench, barbell (with plate configuration), dumbbells (with custom weight ranges), kettlebells, cables, pull-up bar, and more. You toggle what you have, specify details like your dumbbell range, and the app only generates workouts using equipment available to you.
What platforms is FitProject available on?+
FitProject is available now on iOS through the App Store. Android is not released yet. The app is free, and the interface is available in English and French.
