# FitProject

**A free, privacy-first gym workout log with Smart Rotation — adaptive routines built from your equipment, goals, and training history.**

No subscription. No ads. No data selling. Just a workout app that works.

## What is FitProject?

FitProject is a mobile workout app built for people who want to train consistently without paying $15–30/month for basic features. It started as a personal tool — built by a developer who wanted a simple, private way to log workouts and get smart routines — and is now available for everyone on iOS.

## Features

**Workout Logger** — Log sets, reps, weight, and rest periods with a fast interface designed for use at the gym. Track warm-up sets separately and see your personal records for every exercise.

**Smart Rotation** — Adaptive routines built from your equipment, goals (strength, hypertrophy, endurance), weekly frequency (2–7 days), session duration (15–120 min), and training history. It generates balanced push/pull/legs splits. It is a rule-based engine that runs on your phone: same inputs, same workout. No AI, no black box.

**Progress Tracking** — Strength curves over time, personal records, weekly volume, active training time, and weekly streaks. Real data from real sessions.

**Equipment Configuration** — Tell the app exactly what you have: dumbbells with weight ranges, barbells with plates, cables, bench, pull-up bar, and more. Workouts only use equipment available to you.

**Reps in Reserve (RIR)** — Rate your effort after each exercise from max effort to very easy. The app uses this to understand your intensity and adjust future suggestions.

**Custom Templates** — Create your own workout templates, save completed workouts as templates, or build a weekly routine from scratch. Use Smart Rotation when you want it, skip it when you don't.

## Privacy

FitProject is privacy-first. Your workout data is not sold to advertisers, shared with third parties, or used for tracking. There are no analytics trackers, no ads, no data brokers. Your fitness data is yours alone.

## Why Free?

This isn't a startup. There's no venture capital, no monetization plan, no premium tier waiting behind a paywall. FitProject is a passion project built by someone who uses it every day and wants to share it with others who feel the same frustration with overpriced fitness apps.
